## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM**: Partially slop. Dark navy + saturated indigo is a first-order category reflex. Three patterns smell strongly of AI-generated SaaS: Exo 2 italic uppercase logo wordmark, Lucide HandshakeIcon as brand mark, side-stripe orange warning border (impeccable hard ban).

**Deterministic scan**: Unavailable. detect.mjs reports "bundled detector not found".

**Visual overlays**: Not used (detector unavailable).

## Overall Impression

Competent product UI grafted onto a branded surface that wants to be bolder. Trade math, validation, and information architecture are genuinely good. What's missing is the connective tissue: a first-time user has no idea what "Try Trade" vs "Generate Trades" means. The user's three concerns (font, color, logo) are all valid and re-validated by the live inspection. Biggest opportunity: use the empty state and disabled-button state to teach the product.

## What's Working

1. **Player card row composition.** Avatar + bold name + subtle position/age + salary + contract length is dense without feeling cramped.
2. **Cap math display in trade-result view.** Outgoing/Incoming/Cap Difference with semantic green/red, plus Updated Team Cap Info table, is the level of detail your capologist audience expects.
3. **Error copy is professional-grade.** "Indiana Pacers (1st apron): incoming exceeds limit by $3.9M — need $50.2M max" is specific, technical, and actionable.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Users can't tell what "Try Trade" vs "Generate Trades" does
**Why it matters**: The user's stated concern. Lowest score on heuristic table (#6 and #10). Choke point for first-time activation.
**Fix**: Empty state becomes the lesson with two-column "Generate Trades" / "Try a Trade" explainers. Disabled-state hovers explain why a button is disabled.
**Commands**: `/impeccable clarify`, `/impeccable onboard`.

### [P0] The brand surface (font + logo + primary color) is AI-slop tier
**Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md says "beautiful enough to live on my second monitor"; none of these elements is doing that. First-order category reflex.
**Fix**: Drop Exo 2 italic for an editorial/serif or considered grotesque. Pivot indigo to lightish green (user's instinct). Drop handshake icon; go pure wordmark or commission a custom mark.
**Commands**: `/impeccable typeset`, `/impeccable colorize`, custom logo work.

### [P1] Adding a second team silently wipes prior asset selections
**Why it matters**: Capologist persona will hit this within minutes. Heuristic #3 failure.
**Fix**: Preserve asset selections across team additions, or surface a confirmation toast.
**Command**: `/impeccable harden`.

### [P1] AI-generated and user-tried result views are visually identical
**Why it matters**: Emotionally different moments treated the same way. Misses brand opportunity; AI trades are your content engine and should feel like a found gem.
**Fix**: Differentiate at the surface — AI view gets "Generated by Claude" tag + one-line rationale; user-tried view leads with validation verdict and "Save / Refine" prominence.
**Commands**: `/impeccable shape`, `/impeccable craft`.

### [P2] Side-stripe orange warning banner is on the impeccable hard-ban list
**Why it matters**: Pattern is the same cue every alert-component-in-every-SaaS uses. Conflicts with Kinetic Ledger no-line ethos.
**Fix**: Replace stripe with full tinted-background treatment, leading icon, bolder copy. Also fixes DESIGN.md's success-state "2px left-accent" pattern for consistency.
**Commands**: `/impeccable polish`, or fold into `colorize`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex the Capologist**: Hits cap math, professional error copy, "from IND/BOS" provenance. Breaks: second-team selection wipe, invalid scenarios as headlines, no keyboard shortcuts.

**Jordan the First-Timer**: Hits "OVER CAP" chip, fast team picker. Breaks: "Try Trade vs Generate Trades" mystery, "+" → menu non-obviousness, "Cap Space -$39.3M" reads as deficit.

**Marcus the Screenshot-poster**: Hits pagination, Save/Snapshot buttons. Breaks: Snapshot not differentiated from Save, AI result view lacks "shareable card" energy.

## Minor Observations

- Player avatars intermittently render as gray placeholders. Show team-color monogram during load.
- Team header "swap" icon has no label; tooltip needed.
- "1 Player/Pick Selected" pill is tiny; could earn a bottom-dock pattern.
- "OVER CAP" yellow chip implies a problem; consider neutral metadata treatment.
- Right-panel "Add Another Team" copy tries to teach both flows in one sentence; split or remove.
- Footer-zone help text is the only help surface and deserves more presence.
- Invalid AI trades surface as first results; should be sorted last and clearly tagged.

## Questions to Consider

- What if the empty state did the entire job of explaining the product before the user clicks anything?
- What if "Generate Trades" was the headline action and "Try Trade" was discovered later?
- What would the AI-result card look like if you treated each scenario like a magazine card, not a form result?
- What if the brand pivoted to a single distinctive serif wordmark with no icon?
- What if "OVER CAP" was a neutral team-state badge alongside "UNDER CAP" / "1ST APRON" / "2ND APRON"?
